Moin,

I'm a Fullstack Developer based in Leipzig, Germany. I specialize in developing robust and scalable web applications and software solutions. I graduated from Martin-Luther-University Halle-Wittenberg with bachelor's and master's degree in computer science, specialising in algorithm engineering and optimisation algorithms. As a software engineer, I enjoy bridging the gap between technical engineering and real processes in the field.

Through my background, I have developed a strong intercultural understanding. Growing up in multiple countries and participating in a student exchange program gave me the opportunity to experience diverse environments firsthand. My volunteer work and regular interactions with expats have allowed me to both share my own insights and learn from others. In 2021, I gave an [interview](/assets/Blick%20-%20MZ.pdf).

If I'm not in front of a computer screen, I'm probably working on my Spanish, playing indie games on the Steam Deck, or reading books about Zamonia.
