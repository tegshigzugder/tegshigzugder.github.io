#### Chess

> **_"All that matters on the chessboard is good moves."_**

I’ve been playing chess since childhood. Lately, I’ve also started enjoying reading chess books and studying classic games to understand the strategic ideas of brilliant players. These days, I’m playing Rapid (10-minute) games and actively aiming to reach a 1500 rating.

#### History

> **_"Эх түүхээ мэдэхгүй хүн ойд төөрсөн сармагчин лугаа адил."_**

"A person who does not know their history is like a monkey lost in the forest."

I have developed a keen interest in the historical events that shape a nation. The history of Mongolia, in particular, feels both tangible and vast to me. To gain a proper understanding of our heritage and the world around us, I have started reading the book "Монгол Улсын Түүх" on Mongolian history, published by the "Монгол Улсын Их Сургууль". I am also preparing a presentation on the book.

#### Marathon

> **_"Der Schmerz geht. Der Stolz bleibt._**

"The pain goes away. The pride remains."

I’m an avid runner and started using Strava/Garmin to track my runs. In April 2025, I achieved one of my long-term goals by completing the [47th Leipzig Marathon](/assets/Certificate%20-%2047th%20Leipzig%20Marathon.pdf). I’m currently training for a sub-4 finish at the 25th Dresden Marathon in November 2025. I enjoy both the physical and mental challenges that running brings.

#### Photography

> **_"Inspiration exists, but it has to find you working."_**

I recently developed a passion for photography and am deeply interested in both the technical aspects of capturing great photos and the post-processing techniques. Currently, I’m dedicating time to practice by taking as many photos as possible and becoming familiar with the concepts.

#### Spanish

> **_"Vale, vale."_**

Arriving in Spain for my Erasmus programme without speaking a word of Spanish, I was immersed in the language from day one. Everyday situations, whether grocery shopping or buying some bizcocho from the local bakery, quickly made Spanish necessary. By interacting with the locals and building relationships through speaking Spanish, I began to truly enjoy the language. This experience sparked a real passion for it. Today I'm preparing for the DELE exam to take my Spanish to the next level.

#### Volunteer

> **_"It’s not a year in your life. It’s a life in a year."_**

After a memorable Erasmus experience in Spain, where I was warmly hosted by the Erasmus Student Network (ESN) Málaga, I felt inspired to give back and joined ESN Halle (e.V.) as a volunteer. I initially served as the section's Webmaster and eventually became the President. My primary focus was on knowledge transfer, where I documented key processes and roles to ensure smooth operations for future volunteers. I also organized events like ESN Insight, which introduced international students to volunteering opportunities in their home sections, and Erasmus Insight, which connected returning Erasmus students with those about to embark on their journey.
