# Explore Note

An experience-sharing hub that makes documenting and sharing your travel and dining experiences a breeze, with no need to create an account. Whether you're trying new restaurants or exploring new sights, ExploreNote allows you to effortlessly review points of interest and capture your thoughts on the go. It also doubles as your personal journal, helping you track past visits, favorite dishes, and unforgettable moments.

After writing down your experiences, sharing your insights with friends and family is just as easy. With a simple message, they can view your recommendations instantly—no sign-up required. Whether you're curating your own collection of favorite spots or helping others discover theirs, ExploreNote is the perfect companion: simple, private, and shareable. Start exploring and documenting your journey today!

#### Scope

- Primary Scenario: I enjoy discovering new places, capturing photos, and sharing these experiences with friends. However, while I often take pictures and short notes, I don't always create memorable reviews of these places.
- Objective: I want a platform where I can document my experiences without the need for logging in or signing up. This platform should allow me to review places privately, share with friends, or even connect with strangers.

#### Technical Details

- Backend: The backend is built with C# and .NET 8.0, using SQLite and Entity Framework Core. I am also enhancing my Java skills, so the API is being rewritten in Java.
- Frontend: The frontend is developed with TypeScript using Vue 3 and the Quasar framework. As I continue to enhance my Angular skills, I’m also planning to rewrite the app in Angular.
