In April 2025, I completed my first marathon.
It was an experience I had eagerly anticipated.
The first half went well, and I was happy to finish it in two hours.
However, during the last 10 kilometers, I experienced cramps in both legs, which significantly slowed me down.
Despite this, I was surprised to feel a remarkable sense of clarity and determination to finish the race.

A huge thanks to Masha, Tim, the organizers, my fellow runners, and everyone who came out to support.
Also, the spontaneous video by Ronny is at the bottom of the page.
It was a fantastic event to be part of.

Through the marathon, I gained the following insights:

- Getting water from the stands wasn't as simple and often disrupted my running rhythm.
- Dehydration or undernourishment after the 30 km mark seems inevitable without proper planning.
- Maintaining a consistent running pace for extended periods is crucial.

My recovery took quite a while. Therefore, for future marathons:

- Explore the benefits of barefoot shoes to strengthen the legs.
- Maintain greater consistency in the marathon training.
- Take the nutritional aspect more seriously, aiming to understand how my body's needs change every 10 kilometers.
